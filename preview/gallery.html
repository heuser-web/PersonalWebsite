Gallery

Reno, the pines, and the SAE house. All my own photographs.

Three photographs, all mine: the city I live in now, what I do on a weekend outside it, and the people I spend it with.

What you are looking at

  1. Reno, after dark. Two sides of Nevada in one frame: casino chandeliers overhead and a Reno sunset past them. I moved here from Las Vegas for school, and the sunsets are the thing I did not expect to love most.
  2. Treenet. Rigging a treenet on a camping trip with friends. A treenet is a woven rope platform strung high between trees, part hammock and part climbing project, and the best seat at any campsite. I make them.
  3. Dye is Life. A Saturday at the Sigma Alpha Epsilon house. I am a brother of SAE, and this is the chapter at its loudest: a full yard, a full roof, and one table everyone is watching.

Every photo on this page is my own. Nothing here is stock, and nothing was pulled off the internet, which matters both for copyright and because the point of a gallery is that it is actually yours.
