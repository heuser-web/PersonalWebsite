What I am building

Three products, one habit: ship it, then make it good.

I would rather have something running badly on a real server than something perfect in a document. Every project below started as a weekend idea and ended up as software other people actually use.

Founder

GoMojo

A free AI concierge for Las Vegas. You give it a vibe and a budget; six seconds later you have a real three-stop night with real venues, real times and real costs. Behind the answer is a seven-agent swarm that argues about budget, distance, timing and taste before it commits.

  • 7-agent AI swarm
  • Installable PWA
  • Free tier, no ads
  • Reno + Sparks next

Frontend Design Lead

TagData

A personal-data vault I was hired to redesign, with full authority over the frontend. The TagData ID turns any slice of your vault into a styled, scannable card — and the shared data lives inside the link itself, never on a server.

  • $3.5M invested
  • Full frontend authority
  • Token-driven design system
  • Next.js + .NET

Builder

GoInvest

A multi-agent research sandbox where eight investor personas — momentum, quant, macro, value, biotech, forensic short — debate a ticker, vote, and log every argument and outcome to a database. Educational only, and deliberately paper-traded.

  • 8 investor personas
  • Every debate logged
  • Paper trading only
  • Not investment advice
