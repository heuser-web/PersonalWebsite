Why it exists

Nobody knows what you own except you.

Almost every estate is put back together afterward, by people who are grieving, working from a filing cabinet and a guessed password. LifeSwitch is the same work, done in advance.

What it does

  • Snap and seal Photograph anything you own. It gets a fingerprint and a number nobody can guess, so you can prove the photo has never changed.
  • One vault Passports, policies, accounts, deeds and logins, each on a form that knows what matters and when it expires.
  • Who gets what Your whole estate on one page, with a name beside every asset, down to the percent.
  • Share by scan A QR card with only the details you pick. The details travel inside the code, so nothing is stored on a server.

The Switch

Nothing opens while you are here.

You check in every 30 to 180 days. Go quiet and a window of 7 to 60 days begins, and signing in at any point stops it. Your plan is sealed under a key cut in three: your executor, someone you trust, and us. Any two open it. No one can alone.

Release turns on once legal review is complete. Until then nothing is sent. LifeSwitch never holds or moves your money, and it is not a will.
