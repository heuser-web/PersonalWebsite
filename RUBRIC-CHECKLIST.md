## Professional Design — 30 pts

### Quality site with customized theme — 15 pts
- [ ] Theme is **not** a "Twenty…" theme → you are running **Heuser**, custom-built
- [ ] The theme is clearly customized, not a default template
- [ ] Colors, fonts, header, footer and layout are yours
- [ ] Site is readable on a phone → test it on your actual phone
- [ ] No broken images, no placeholder text, no `((...))` markers left anywhere
- [ ] Opening crawl plays, and **Skip intro** works — a grader must never be trapped in it
- [ ] Every required page is reachable within one click of the hero

### Custom menu in order — 8 pts
- [ ] A **custom menu** was created under Appearance → Menus (not the auto page list)
- [ ] Assigned to the **Primary Menu** location
- [ ] Order is exactly: **Home | About Me | Resume | Gallery | Blog**
- [ ] Any extra page (Excel Helper) comes **after** Blog

### Website-relevant widgets — 7 pts
- [ ] No Search, Recent Posts, Recent Comments, Archives, Categories or Meta widgets
      *(the theme unregisters most of these for you)*
- [ ] Footer widget areas hold something relevant — Explore menu, contact details
- [ ] No empty or default widget areas showing WordPress furniture

## Blog — 30 pts

### 1 blog post — 14 pts
- [ ] Post is published (a **Post**, not a Page)
- [ ] Topic is a hoax, scam, or data manipulation ✔ review manipulation
- [ ] Explains **the purpose** — what someone was trying to accomplish ✔
- [ ] Explains **how it worked** — how it was executed ✔
- [ ] Explains **how to tell real from fake** ✔ five concrete checks
- [ ] Well over the one-paragraph minimum ✔ ~1,130 words
- [ ] "Hello world!" default post deleted

### 4 comments — 8 pts
- [ ] 4 comments from 4 **different real people**
- [ ] All 4 are **approved** and visible on the live post

### 4 responses — 8 pts
- [ ] You replied to **each** of the 4 comments
- [ ] Replies are **threaded** under their comment
- [ ] Replies show the **Author** badge (posted while logged in)
- [ ] Each reply says something specific — not four copies of "thanks!"
